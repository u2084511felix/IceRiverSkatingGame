Initial ideas:

3d (first / third) person river ice skating game. 

The core game challenge will relate to the theme of "Changes" - The ice skater will be skating in early spring somewhere in some northern country.
Parts of the river will be too thin to skate on. These will be visually noticable as slightly clearer patches on the river.
The objective will be to to avoid skating over these patches to avoid falling into the icy river.

Core mechanics.


Movement mechanics:
WASD {
    w = skate forward.
    a = lean left.
    s = break.
    d = lean right.
}

W + (A/D) lean + stepover (left/right).
S + (A/D) skid lead foot.
Shift + S = two footed hard break.

SPACE = Jump
TAB = toggle crouch (increases speed)
